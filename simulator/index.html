Hardware

The edition changes only the startup animation; picking one replays it. The firmware only looks for the ADC at boot, so power-cycle after changing it. Calibration survives a power cycle, as it does in the device's flash.

Things to try

    
    
      
  1. On first boot the status line says NOT CALIBRATED. With the gas set to Air, swipe (drag) left to the MENU, tap CALIBRATE, then 1-POINT. It samples until the reading is stable, then shows AIR SAVED.
    2. 
      
  2. Switch the gas to EAN32. The ring turns orange and the status reads SETTLING while the cell responds, then STABLE once readings hold within 0.15 % for 2 s. The MOD line shows depths at PO₂ 1.4 and 1.6.
    3. 
      
  3. In the menu, tap DETAILS to turn it on. A details page then sits between the main screen and the menu, with raw millivolts, the calibration points, the linearity and the battery voltage. The setting is remembered across power cycles.
    4. 
      
  4. Drop the sensor output below 7 mV and recalibrate to see the weak-sensor warning. Untick the ADS1115 and power-cycle to see NO ADC.
    5. 
      
  5. For a 2-point calibration, choose 2-POINT. After AIR SAVED, switch the gas to O₂ and tap START O2. It captures the O₂ point and shows the linearity: LIN is the O₂/air ratio as a % of ideal, with R² of the fit through zero, air and O₂. Lower the linearity slider to 96 % or 90 % and repeat to see it graded orange or red.
